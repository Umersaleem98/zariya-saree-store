# Rang Mahal typography — English storefront

The website uses English and left-to-right layout. Its content celebrates Pakistani culture, traditions, Eid gatherings, weddings and craft without claiming that sample products are handmade or locally manufactured.

| Section | Font family | Style / weight |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Rang Mahal logo | Cormorant Garamond | Regular 400, spaced letters |
| Logo tagline | DM Sans | Regular 400, uppercase |
| Moving announcement | DM Sans | Regular 400, uppercase |
| Sticky category navigation | DM Sans | Semibold 600 |
| Hero and main section headings | Cormorant Garamond | Regular 400; selected phrases italic |
| Category and product names | Cormorant Garamond | Regular 400 |
| Descriptions, forms and footer links | DM Sans | Regular 400 |
| Eyebrow labels | DM Sans | Medium 500, uppercase |
| Main buttons | DM Sans | Semibold 600 |
| Product prices | DM Sans | Medium 500 |
| Story signatures and captions | Cormorant Garamond | Italic 400 |
| Admin headings / figures | Cormorant Garamond | Regular 400 / Medium 500 |
| Admin controls and tables | DM Sans | Regular 400–500 |

Local header height: 125px desktop, 103px mobile. Announcement height: 39px desktop, 34px mobile. Category row: 59px desktop, 51px mobile, sticky at the top when scrolling.

Fallbacks: Georgia/serif and Arial/sans-serif. Source: resources/css/app.css.
